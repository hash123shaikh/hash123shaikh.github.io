I build AI that can tell, before treatment, how a head and neck cancer will behave.

I am a data scientist in the Quantitative Imaging Research and AI Lab (QIRAIL) at CMC Vellore. My work covers radiomics, tumour segmentation and the pipelines that move imaging data from hospital archives into models people can trust.

I also test whether published models hold up. One of my studies reproduced a CNN prognosis paper that reported 95% checklist compliance and found AUCs off by up to 0.22.

I am looking for a PhD in AI for oncology: prognosis, recurrence, toxicity or follow-up, with AI at the centre.
