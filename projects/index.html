Projects

Radiomics risk stratification in head and neck cancer

ESTRO 2026 poster highlight

  • Built a 10-feature signature (2 clinical, 8 radiomic) to predict locoregional recurrence.
  • Compared 5 metaheuristic algorithms, including a genetic algorithm, over 1,000+ candidate features.
  • AUC 0.82 (95% CI 0.62–0.95) in 327 patients treated with primary radiation, with SHAP explanations for each prediction.

scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy

Reproducibility study of a CNN prognosis model

Under submission, Scientific Reports

  • Re-ran the authors’ released code, data and Docker container. AUCs differed from the published ones by −0.22 to +0.10, and fixing the code issues took almost seven weeks.
  • External validation on a separate cohort (n=102) gave an AUC of 0.49–0.59, close to chance.
  • Scored the paper against CLAIM and TRIPOD+AI: high checklist compliance did not mean it was reproducible.

CT tumour segmentation with 3D CNNs

With NIT Karnataka, Surathkal

  • Curated 167 CT cases (137 MAASTRO, 30 CMC Vellore) and trained a 3D nnU-Net for gross tumour volume. Dice was 0.62 on MAASTRO, 0.63 on CMC and 0.65 combined.
  • Co-author of AGSR-Net, which lifts Dice from 0.70 to 0.73 and cuts HD95 from 33.92 to 21.22 mm across 896 patients.

nnU-Net, 3D Slicer, ITK-SNAP
